1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Oceanside Network LLC ("Oceanside," "we") collects, uses and shares information when you visit our website, listen to or watch our shows, or contact us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us: name, email, company, show details and messages you send through forms or email.

Listening and viewing data: when you download or stream an episode, our hosting and ad-delivery providers receive technical information such as IP address, user agent (the app or device used), time and approximate location derived from IP address. This is standard for podcast measurement and ad delivery.

Website data: pages viewed, referring pages, device and browser type, collected through cookies or similar technologies where used.

We use information to: operate and improve the Services; respond to inquiries; measure audience size and engagement; deliver, cap and measure advertising, including dynamically inserted ads; prevent fraud and abuse; and comply with law.
