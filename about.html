The ocean side of the street

Ocean City is a thin barrier island: the Atlantic on one side, the bay on the other, Coastal Highway up the middle. At 56th Street, the ocean side is where the summers happened. Early mornings on the sand, long days with the radio on, the whole town filling up every June with people from everywhere.

Growing up there teaches you a few things. A small place can reach a lot of people. A good story travels. And the busy season pays for the whole year, so be ready when the crowds show up. [ADD A PERSONAL MEMORY OR TWO]

Oceanside brings that to podcasting and streaming: a network that takes a show everywhere its audience is, sells it like a real media company, and keeps the people who make it at the center.
