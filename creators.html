What's included

Everything a show needs to grow, in five parts.

01

Hosting

Audio and video hosting built for growth.

  • One home for your audio and video feeds
  • Migration from your current host, handled
  • Analytics across every platform in one dashboard
02

Monetization

Direct and programmatic revenue, stacked.

  • Direct sales: our team sells sponsorships for your show
  • Indirect programmatic demand across the network
  • Baked-in personal endorsements
  • Dynamically inserted brand, producer and host reads
03

Distribution & promotion

Get heard, then get found.

  • Distribution to every major platform
  • Cross-promotion across the network
  • Guest booking and guest hosting
  • Feed drops and feed swaps
04

Content strategy

A plan for what to make next, and why.

  • Show positioning and format
  • Episode planning and release cadence
  • Titles, thumbnails and packaging
  • Regular performance reviews
05

Production resources & support

Help behind the mic and the camera.

  • Editing, mixing and mastering
  • Video production and clip creation
  • Recording setup and remote support
  • Artwork and show assets

Two engines of revenue

Direct sales bring premium sponsors to your show. Programmatic demand fills the rest of your inventory, across new episodes and your entire back catalog.

Direct

Sponsorships sold by our team, with your approval on every brand.

Programmatic

Automated demand that keeps every episode earning.

Baked-in endorsements

Recorded into the episode in the host's own voice. Permanent, personal and part of the show.

Host reads

Recorded by the host and inserted dynamically, so they can run across new and past episodes.

Producer reads

Voiced by the show's producer, for scale without taking host time.

Brand reads

Produced by the advertiser and inserted dynamically across the network.

Growth, built in

The network is the promotion engine.

Guest booking

We book relevant guests from across the network and beyond.

Guest hosting

Our creators appear on each other's shows to share audiences.

Feed drops

A full episode of a network show drops into your feed, and yours into theirs.

Feed swaps

Trade promos with shows whose listeners will love yours.

Distribution, optimized

Distribution is tuned for performance across three measures that matter.

Views

Titles, thumbnails, chapters and clips tuned for each platform's discovery.

Engagement

Formats, lengths and release timing that keep audiences listening and watching.

Revenue

Ad load, placement and demand managed to raise earnings per episode.
