Two engines of revenue

Direct sales bring premium sponsors to your show. Programmatic demand fills the rest of your inventory, across new episodes and your entire back catalog.

Direct

Sponsorships sold by our team, with your approval on every brand.

Programmatic

Automated demand that keeps every episode earning.

Baked-in endorsements

Recorded into the episode in the host's own voice. Permanent, personal and part of the show.

Host reads

Recorded by the host and inserted dynamically, so they can run across new and past episodes.

Producer reads

Voiced by the show's producer, for scale without taking host time.

Brand reads

Produced by the advertiser and inserted dynamically across the network.
